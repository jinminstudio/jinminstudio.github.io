Jinmin Studio ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Protect the Bakery (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The Game has no sign-up, and we do not run our own servers. We never receive your name, contact details, or payment card numbers. Your game progress is stored on your device.

If we add cloud saves (Google Play Games), your save data will be stored in your Google account while you use the feature. We will update this policy when that happens.

2. How long information is kept

  • We do not run servers, so we do not keep a separate copy of this information.
  • Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.
  • Auto backup data is kept in your Google account. You can turn it off or delete it in your device's Google backup settings.
  • Information handled by Google and Cloudflare for ads, payments, and time checks is kept under their own policies.

4. Advertising ID and personalized ads

The Game uses your device's advertising ID to show ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads). In some regions, the Game asks for your ad consent the first time you open it.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information was processed, contact us and we will help.

6. Your rights and choices

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. Because we do not store your information, you can handle most of this yourself by uninstalling the Game or changing your device settings. For information processed by Google or Cloudflare, you can contact them directly. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have additional rights under local law; contact us and we will respond.

7. Security

  • Game data saved on your device is encrypted.
  • Network connections use encryption (HTTPS).
  • We design features so the Game does not receive information it does not need.
