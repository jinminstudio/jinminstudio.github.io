Jinmin Studio ("we") publishes the drop rates and costs of the random items in Protect the Bakery (the "Game") on this page. The same information is available in the [Rates] screen of each pull screen inside the Game.

If any rate changes, we will announce it in advance on this page and in the Game's notices before it takes effect. Each rate below applies identically to every single pull, and earlier results do not affect later ones (the Divine Beast pity counter is explained separately below).

1. Capsule type

1-1. Hero Pull

Cost: 150 gems per pull, 1,350 gems per 10-pull (10% discount). You can also pull with Hero Pull Tickets; 1 ticket = 1 pull (a 10-pull uses 10 tickets). The rates below are the same when you use tickets.

The Game first checks for a Divine Beast card. If none comes up, one of 5 Hero card types comes up. Hero cards have no grades, and the quantity is always 1.

The 5 Hero card types split the remaining 99.95% (when no Divine Beast comes up) equally: 99.95% ÷ 5 = 19.99%. Baekho is currently the only Divine Beast that can be pulled. If more Divine Beasts are added, we will add rows to this table and announce it in advance.

10-pull guarantee

  • In a Hero 10-pull, if you do not own every hero yet but none of the 10 cards is a hero you do not own, the last card is replaced with a card of a hero you do not own (chosen at random).
  • If you already own every hero, the guarantee does not apply.
  • If the last slot is a Divine Beast, that slot is left unchanged and the regular slot before it is replaced instead. If all 10 slots are Divine Beasts, nothing is replaced.
  • Mercenary Pulls and Gold Pulls have no 10-pull guarantee.

Divine Beast pity

  • Each Divine Beast has its own counter. Every Hero Pull (gems, tickets and 10-pulls all count as 1 pull each) raises the counter of each Divine Beast you have not obtained by 1, and obtaining that Divine Beast resets its counter to 0.
  • Baekho is guaranteed on the 1,000th Hero Pull. Before that, each pull has a 0.05% chance.
  • Reaching the pity takes at most 1,000 pulls, which is at most 150,000 gems (135,000 gems if all are 10-pulls).
  • The counter is saved with your game data and persists when you close and reopen the app.

For reference, the chance of obtaining Baekho naturally before the pity is about 4.9% within 100 pulls, 13.9% within 300 pulls, 22.1% within 500 pulls and 39.3% within 999 pulls. Including the pity rule, the average number of pulls needed is about 787. These figures are reference values calculated from the rules above.

1-2. Mercenary Pull

Cost: 20 gems per pull, 180 gems per 10-pull. The result is first split into a complete card (20%) or a fragment (80%), and then one of 7 classes is chosen with equal probability. The quantity is always 1 (one card or one fragment).

ResultRate per pull
1 complete card: Warrior · Archer · Mage · Lancer · Rogue · Medic · Cheerleader2.86% each (= 20% ÷ 7)
1 fragment: Warrior · Archer · Mage · Lancer · Rogue · Medic · Cheerleader11.43% each (= 80% ÷ 7)
Total100.00%
  • 10 fragments of the same class = 1 card, and fragments are converted into a card automatically the moment you get them.
  • The rates are rounded to two decimal places, so adding up the values on screen may show 100.03% (2.86 × 7 + 11.43 × 7). The sum before rounding is exactly 100%.

1-3. Gold Pull (Mercenary)

Cost: 3,000 gold per pull, 5 pulls per day (the count resets when the date changes). The result rates are the same table as the Mercenary Pull (1-2). There is no 10-pull and no guarantee.
